What is the importance of the year 2019?
Just before the Covid-19 pandemic had infiltrated the world, 2019 was a time that demonstrated peak standards of living. Many like to recall the memories that existed during this time period as it captured a rare convergence of peak-optimism. It has become Gen Z's 1999, and the main driver of this 2019 nostalgia is its status as the final year of happiness and social predictability. The events that occurred during 2020 and beyond felt sudden and was a painful slap of reality. Therefore, 2019 acts as a psychological safe haven, where everything was still deemed as normal.
